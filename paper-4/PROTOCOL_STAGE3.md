# Paper 4 — Stage 3: power of the ranking null (positive control and a transferable-content ceiling) (frozen before any result)
Committed 2026-10-30, after the second ICML-style review of draft v0.5 (overall 4/10). Its main objection (W1): the ranking null is uninformative unless the protocol is shown able to detect content-dependent personalisation where it exists, and unless the real data's transferable rater-specific direction signal is bounded. Any change after the first result is logged under "Amendments".

## S3-P: semi-synthetic positive control (MultiPref)
**Generator.** The real MultiPref panel (same items, raters, which rater rated which item, folds, validation groups and k-shot draws as stage 2a/2c). A generator model — the stage-2a D head plus DFs offsets, fitted once on *all* ratings (generator only; never used by a learner) — gives each observed (rater, item) a 5-level distribution P_ri. Synthetic labels: y_ri ~ softmax(log P_ri + c_ri · [−2, −1, 0, 1, 2]) with a content term c_ri = θ_r · g_i, where
- g_i = two standardised item features of the response pair, fixed now: (a) log length ratio of response B to response A (characters); (b) the projection of (E_B − E_A) (the per-response embeddings of stage 2c, PCA-256 space) on a unit direction drawn at random (seed 0) and orthogonalised to the ridge direction (λ = 1,000) that predicts the item's mean rating from (E_B − E_A), so that (b) is not the consensus quality axis;
- θ_r ~ N(0, τ² I₂) per rater (seed 1), independent of everything else.

**Strength.** τ is set by simulation of the generator only (no learner is fitted for this) so that the *generator-oracle* direction accuracy — knowing θ_r and the generator — exceeds the generator without the content term by +2 and +5 points on the test ratings of all folds; plus τ = 0 (null control). Three synthetic panels: τ₀ = 0, τ₂, τ₅.

**Learners and protocol.** Unchanged from the paper: D, DFs, DL (few-shot-tuned prior, Stage 2 Amendment 5) via factorised_rm.py; Dp, DFsp, VPL, PAL-B (K = 2) via vpl_pal.py on graded labels (Stage 2c Amendment-2 settings); k ∈ {0, 20, 50}; 5 folds; 5 draws (factorised_rm) / as in stage 2c (vpl_pal).

**Outcomes.** For every learner, its own direction-accuracy gain m(k) − m(0) at k = 20 and 50 (rater bootstrap), and the fraction of the generator-oracle gain it recovers.

**Decision rule (fixed now).** The protocol "has power" for content personalisation of a given strength if at least one content-interacting learner (DL, VPL or PAL-B) shows an own direction gain at k = 20 whose lower 95 % bound exceeds +0.01 — i.e. it would have failed the paper's ranking-null criterion. Sanity: at τ₀ every learner's own direction gain must have a 95 % interval containing a value within ±0.005 of 0; otherwise the control is reported as broken and S3-P is not used. Reporting: if power holds at τ₅ but not τ₂, the paper states the null as "content personalisation that would add ≥ 5 points of direction accuracy for an oracle is excluded; ≈ 2 points is below the detection limit"; if no learner has power at τ₅, the paper states that the ranking null is uninformative about content personalisation of that size with these learners, and the title and abstract are revised accordingly.

## S3-C: transferable rater-specific direction signal on the real data (MultiPref)
For each test rater of each fold (stage-2a splits), the rater's test-item ratings are split in half at random (10 splits, seed 2). Per-rater content weights (ridge on the top-32 principal components of E_B − E_A, penalty chosen once by the same procedure on the training raters' training items) are fitted to the rater's residual direction relative to the frozen D head on one half (offset logit c · [−2, −1, 0, 1, 2], as in the generator) and evaluated on the other half: direction-accuracy gain over D, averaged over halves and splits, rater bootstrap. The same statistic is computed on the τ₀, τ₂, τ₅ synthetic panels as calibration. Reported as the transferable-content ceiling of the real data relative to the synthetic strengths (e.g. "the real data look like τ < τ₂"). No hypothesis; descriptive.

## Forbidden statements
"Users have no content preferences"; anything about datasets other than MultiPref from S3; any claim from S3-P if its τ₀ sanity check fails.

## Amendments

**Amendment 1 (2026-10-30 16:00, after seeing one τ0 fold of the per-response learners — VPL own direction gain +0.012 [+0.003, +0.021] with no content signal — and before any τ2/τ5 result).** The own-gain contrast m(k) − m(0) is biased for learners whose k = 0 state is not the population state (VPL starts from z = 0, which the real data already showed to be a poor point: its k = 0 likelihood is far below the text-only model). Any k ratings then improve it, whoever they come from. Added for every learner and k > 0: a **permutation control** — after adaptation, the test raters' adapted rater-side parameters (offsets, low-rank latent, VPL z, PAL weights) are reassigned by a random derangement among the test raters of the same fold, k and draw (seed 777 + 10,000·fold + 100·k + draw), and each rater is evaluated with another rater's parameters. **Primary S3 contrast** (replaces the own gain in the power rule and the τ0 sanity check): m(own k ratings) − m(another rater's k ratings), direction accuracy at k = 20, rater bootstrap. The own gain stays reported. All S3-P runs are restarted with this output (the partial runs are discarded; no τ2/τ5 output had been written).

**Amendment 2 (2026-10-30 18:00, after the factorised S3-P results — DL with the few-shot-tuned prior shows no own-vs-other direction gain even at τ5, its tuned adaptation prior is the grid maximum 8192 in almost every fold and k — and before any per-response τ2/τ5 result).** Exploratory diagnostic, not part of the power rule (which stays on the pre-specified tuned-prior DL): DL is also run with the training-rater prior (the Stage-2 primary prior; no `--adapt_tune`), same panels, folds, k, draws and permutation control (tag `_trp_f*`), to tell whether the absence of power comes from the prior selection shrinking the latent to the population or from the model not representing the injected content preference. Reported, labelled exploratory.

**Amendment 3 (2026-10-30 19:00, after the τ5 per-response result — no content-interacting learner has power at τ5 (DL −0.0002, VPL +0.0001, PAL-B +0.0023 own − other at k = 20) — and before the τ2 per-response result).** Pre-registered consequence: the paper states that the ranking null is uninformative about content personalisation of that size with these learners, and the title and abstract are revised. Exploratory dose-response (not part of any decision rule): two stronger panels, τ10 and τ20, calibrated the same way to a generator-oracle gain of +10 and +20 points (same θ draws, features and Gumbel noise), run with the same learners, settings and permutation control, to locate the strength at which these learners start to recover injected content preferences. Reported, labelled exploratory.

**Amendment 4 (2026-10-30 20:25, before any real-data run with the permutation control).** To compare the real data with the synthetic dose-response on the same contrast, the real MultiPref runs are repeated with `--perm_control`, k ∈ {0, 20, 50}, settings unchanged: vpl_pal.py (Dp, DFsp, VPL, PAL-B K = 2; Stage 2c Amendment-2 settings; tag `_perm`) and factorised_rm.py (D, DFs, DL with the few-shot-tuned prior; tag `_A5perm_f*`). Reported: own − other direction accuracy at k = 20 and 50 for each learner on the real data, placed on the synthetic dose-response curve (τ0 … τ20) to state the largest injected content-preference strength that the real-data result is consistent with. Descriptive, no new decision rule.

**Amendment 5 (2026-10-30 23:30, answering review W2 (feature bottleneck); before any run with these features).** The per-response learners (Dp, DFsp, VPL, PAL-B K = 2) are re-run with the full 3,584-dimensional last-token states instead of their label-free PCA-256 (each dimension standardised; `vpl_pal.py --pca 0`), everything else unchanged except the D-family ridge grid, extended to {100, 300, 1,000, 3,000, 10,000} because the input dimension is 14× larger. Panels: real MultiPref and the synthetic τ5, τ10, τ20; k ∈ {0, 20, 50}; permutation control. Pre-specified reading: the PCA bottleneck explains part of the detection limit if, at τ5 or τ10, VPL or PAL-B with full features has an own − other lower bound above +1 point at k = 20 (it had not with PCA-256). On the real data the own − other contrast is reported for both feature sets; graded LL of every method is reported to check that the full-feature models are not simply worse fitted (their k = 0 LL against the PCA-256 versions).
