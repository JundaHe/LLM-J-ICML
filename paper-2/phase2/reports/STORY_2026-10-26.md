# Revised story after the 2026-10-26 experiments (draft for the user; numbers from RESULTS/PROGRESS, all committed)

## One sentence
**Learned judges can only learn the part of human disagreement that is predictable from the text; on ordinal quality ratings that part is the location (mean) alone, on NLI it includes the shape — so for ordinal judges the annotation budget should buy items, not raters, while raters are needed only where a reliable reference distribution is itself the deliverable (evaluation sets, nominal tasks).**

## The evidence chain (all learner-free or frozen-feature unless stated)
1. **Reproducibility across raters (T9 + reliability cards, 21 panels).** With 3–9 raters the histogram's shape beyond exact mode and mean is net noise for forecasting a new rater (A1x > 0 on all small ordinal panels; with 3 raters there is no shape at all: Crupi, MLCQ support 0 %). Dense ordinal panels (BW, DICES, Dorn) need 20–30 raters before the raw histogram beats a mode+mean comparator; ChaosNLI needs 5–10.
2. **Predictability from text (DECODING_PROTOCOL).** Cross-fitted ridge from the frozen Qwen2.5-7B representation to the panel-1 residual, scored on an independent panel-2: **G ≈ 0 (|G| < 2·10⁻⁵) on six ordinal panels**; **G = +0.0032 [+0.0029, +0.0042] on ChaosNLI MNLI** (permutation control negative, positive controls 0.29–0.65). The same representation that cannot see ordinal disagreement shape sees NLI disagreement shape.
3. **What learners use (audit T2b/T3b/T3c + Gate H).** Replacing the shape of 70–80 % of training targets leaves judges unchanged on ordinal panels (perm − chen ≈ 0) but hurts on ChaosNLI (pzc +0.016–0.052 KL). No target construction — shape shrinkage, location shrinkage, Dirichlet, label smoothing toward uniform or toward the marginal — moves frozen-feature heads by more than ±0.0005 RPS on 11 ordinal panels, with inconsistent signs; only discarding the distribution (hard) costs +0.003–0.010.
4. **Annotation side vs learner side (Gate A vs Gate H).** For *forecasting a new rater from the item's own ratings*, shrinking location (ρ_loc) and shape (λ̂ = X/M) helps substantially (SummEval −0.005, HelpSteer2 −0.012 RPS; the P1-only estimators recover the held-out optimum). For *training a judge* the same targets do nothing: the learner pools location across items and the shape is not in the text.
5. **Budget (budget_heads + LoRA).** Heads: with ≳ 300 items, more items × fewer ratings wins at equal budget (DICES-990 594×1 0.203 < 74×8 0.213); with ≲ 100 items single ratings are too noisy (BW, DICES-350). End-to-end LoRA judges on SummEval coherence: 960 items × 1 rating (960 ratings) RPS 0.1640 and 480 × 2 (960) 0.1648 versus 6,720 ratings 0.1696–0.1701 — one seventh of the annotation, no loss (12 of 42 cells so far).

## Mechanism (being tested; RATER_ITEM_MODEL_PROTOCOL)
Ordered-probit decomposition θ_i (item location) + c_r (rater thresholds) + σ_i (item dispersion). Smoke on BW: thresholds explain almost all held-out likelihood gain (M0 −1.390 → M1 −1.301 → M2 −1.299) and the exact-T reproducible residual X is ≈ 0 under M1 and in the data. Registered prediction for the sweep: on ordinal panels the reproducible shape is thresholds (+ little dispersion), hence not an item property the text could carry; on NLI the shape is interpretive and item-specific.

## What this is and is not
- It is a measurement paper with a mechanism and a decision rule: two reliabilities (across raters, across text), a decomposition that explains the ordinal/nominal split, and a budget rule validated end-to-end.
- It is not a new training objective: Gate H killed that; the shrinkage estimator survives as a tool for building reference distributions (evaluation sets, paper 1's territory).
- Companion to paper 1: evaluation should not average disagreement away (P1); training cannot learn most of it anyway (P2); together: raters for the test set, items for the training set.
