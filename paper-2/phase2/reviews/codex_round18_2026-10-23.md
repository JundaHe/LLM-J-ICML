### A. A1 versus A1x

Your interpretation is substantially right, with one qualification.

- A1 compares the raw histogram with a regularized smoother that need not retain its observed location. Thus A1 mixes the value of location precision and residual allocation. The large negative dense-ordinal A1 values cannot be presented as residual-shape evidence.
- The A1–A1x difference strongly supports the location explanation: at \(a=m-1\), enforcing exact mode/mean changes BW from −0.0138 to +0.0007 and DICES from −0.0046/−0.0017 to approximately zero.
- But this does not identify a purely causal “location component”: the comparators also differ in response source, smoothing, and fallback construction.

A1x is the construct-aligned residual contrast because both forecasts have exactly the same observed mode and mean. It asks whether the empirical residual allocation improves on this particular exact-T forecast. It is not latent residual information or a Bayes-risk difference.

Do not post hoc rename A1x “primary.” Keep A1 as the prespecified primary practical forecast comparison, but make A1x the principal evidence for the narrower mode-and-mean-residual question. Report them jointly. Also, exact-T becomes favorable at \(a=3\) for MNLI but is already favorable at \(a=2\) for SNLI.

### B. What A2 establishes

The “twice residual variance × dose” reading is only an idealized heuristic. Exactly,

\[
E[A2]=\frac{2}{n}E\langle C(h-d),C(p-(h+d)/2)\rangle .
\]

It reduces to approximately twice a destroyed residual covariance only under strong assumptions about conditionally exchangeable donors, equal second moments, and negligible projection distortion.

Therefore A2 \(>0\) establishes that this specific mode-and-mean-preserving donor replacement destroys item-linked information that predicts independent ratings. It does not establish that the information is predictable from text. A2 \(>0\) with learned contrast near zero means: annotation-side signal exists, but the evaluated learner/intervention shows no measurable transmission. It does not distinguish representation, decoder, objective, optimization, or power failures.

Your ridge proposal is the cheapest useful discriminator. On SummEval coherence/relevance first:

1. Repeatedly split ratings into independent panels.
2. Define cumulative residuals relative to the exact-T comparator.
3. In group-cross-fitted item folds, fit nested-CV multi-output ridge from frozen features to panel-1 residuals.
4. Evaluate against panel-2 residuals using both covariance and the proper-score gain
   \[
   2\langle\hat r,r_2\rangle-\|\hat r\|^2.
   \]
5. Symmetrize panel 1/2, refit within the group bootstrap, and include within-T feature permutations.

A positive result demonstrates linearly decodable signal in the existing representation and indicts the training pipeline. A tight null supports only “not linearly decodable from these frozen features,” not “unpredictable from text.”
