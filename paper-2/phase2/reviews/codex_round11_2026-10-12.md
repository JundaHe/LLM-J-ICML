# B. Matching-seed determinism

It narrows, but does not erase, what the three realizations support.

The statement “seeds randomise only within-class donor identity” is not quite correct. The class-to-class transport counts are deterministic, but the algorithm shuffles item identities into those class-level slots. Consequently, the changed-item set and the histogram assigned to a particular item can vary.

I reconstructed the SummEval targets: between seed 1000 and either later seed, only 660–681 of 960 training items retained the same assigned histogram, so roughly 29–31% received a different target. Thus these are genuine item-level intervention realizations, despite identical aggregate dose.

Required wording:

> Within each matching cell, the numbers of assignments between histogram classes—and therefore the changed count and aggregate TV—are deterministic functions of the class sizes. The permutation seed shuffles item identities within these class-level assignment slots, changing which items are altered and their item-level donor assignments, but not the aggregate class flow or dose. Each replication therefore couples a new optimizer realization with a new item-level assignment under the same deterministic class-flow rule. The three realizations cannot separate optimizer from assignment variability and do not test alternative class-pairing rules or dose patterns.

Store and compare both a donor-map hash and an `item → assigned target histogram` hash. A donor-map hash alone is not the scientifically relevant certificate when multiple donors share a histogram.

# D. Acceptable abstract

> Distributional supervision for learned judges is often interpreted as teaching how humans disagree. We test what such learners use by replacing each training histogram with a donor histogram while preserving either its mode or its mode and mean, and by reporting the intervention’s support and total-variation dose. Across seven ordinal panels with frozen-feature heads, exact mode-and-mean-preserving replacement changed average RPS by −0.0024 to +0.0003 relative to original histograms, although several panels had low changed fractions or small perturbations. On SummEval coherence, a higher-dose LoRA intervention (81% changed; mean TV 0.35) yielded a raw RPS contrast of −0.0001 (95% crossed-bootstrap interval [−0.0011, 0.0011]) across three joint optimizer–assignment realizations and three expert folds. Preserving only the mode instead increased RPS by 0.0120 [0.0086, 0.0155], with positive estimates in all nine seed–fold cells. This pattern was not universal: on nominal ChaosNLI, scrambling residual class allocation while preserving modal class and mass increased KL by 0.016–0.029. Soft-label value therefore depended on task and label geometry; these experiments do not support a general claim that judges recover full disagreement distributions.
