## C. Final-dose definition

The target construction matches `probe_nominal.py`, and the reported `TV > 1e-9` values are correct under that exact definition.

My round-13 reconstruction used rowwise inequality after casting both targets to the actual float32 training representation:

```python
np.any(T.astype(np.float32) != hist.astype(np.float32), axis=1)
```

That produces 0.555 / 0.784 / 0.976 / 0.994.

However, I do not recommend the current `TV > 1e-9` fraction as an unqualified dose statistic. `nominal_zc` itself floors probabilities at `1e-9`, and many TVs lie around \(0.8\text{–}2.0\times10^{-9}\). Consequently:

- float32 inequality: 0.555 / 0.784 / 0.976 / 0.994
- TV > \(10^{-9}\): 0.555 / 0.765 / 0.896 / 0.962
- TV > \(10^{-8}\): 0.555 / 0.721 / 0.828 / 0.923

The continuous final TV-over-all-items is stable and matches the stored training certificates to about \(3\times10^{-11}\). I would make mapped fraction plus final TV-all the primary dose, and either omit “final changed” or label it explicitly as an effective-change sensitivity with a threshold safely above the numerical floor.
